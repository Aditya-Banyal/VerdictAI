Our story

Legal clarity, without the fog

VerdictAI began as a university project with one belief: legal documents should be understandable to the people who live with their consequences — students, junior lawyers, and anyone facing a case.

Mission

Turn dense legal text into clear, cited conversations — without replacing professional counsel.

Technology

Today: HTML, CSS, JavaScript. Ahead: React, Node, MongoDB, NLP, LLMs, and RAG — introduced at the right stage.

Privacy-first

Your documents deserve respect. We design for local-first learning now, and secure vaults later.

Vision

A cinematic workspace where opening a contract feels as natural as opening a chat — and every answer points you back to the exact page that matters.

Disclaimer: VerdictAI is for information and research. Not a substitute for a qualified lawyer.
